## Overview

Normalizing flows learn flexible density estimates that are well-suited to anomaly scoring, but the resulting decision surface is opaque. Decision trees are interpretable but inflexible. This paper combines the two — yielding an unsupervised outlier detector that is both accurate and inherently explainable.

The companion repository ships an end-to-end benchmarking pipeline (`main.py`) and a Friedman significance test harness (`friedman.py`) for ranking the methods evaluated in the paper.
