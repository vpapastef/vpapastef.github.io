## Overview

Off-the-shelf anomaly detectors are sensitive to noisy or irrelevant features — a weakness that compounds in unsupervised settings, where you cannot tune to a label. This paper proposes a meta-learning algorithm that runs unsupervised feature selection upstream of standard PyOD detectors, then learns to aggregate their outputs into a more stable ensemble.

The repository ships a reproducible benchmark (`benchmarking.py`) whose results are formatted for the [STAC web platform](https://tec.citius.usc.es/stac/) so a Friedman test can rank methods across datasets.
