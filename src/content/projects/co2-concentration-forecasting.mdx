## Overview

Indoor CO₂ time series mix smooth diurnal trends with sharp event-driven spikes — a regime where neither classical statistical methods nor pure neural sequence models cleanly dominate. This paper proposes a hybrid that pairs ARIMA with the Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT), letting each component handle the part of the signal it captures best.

The companion repository, built on `darts`, runs a head-to-head comparison against a wide baseline panel: NaiveSeasonal, ARIMA, Exponential Smoothing, Theta, FFT, RNN, BlockRNN, TCN, NBEATS, Transformer, and TFT — all reproducible from a single configuration file.
