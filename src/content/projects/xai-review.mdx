## Overview

A wide-ranging survey of machine-learning interpretability: what counts as an explanation, how methods differ in scope (global vs local), in model dependence (model-agnostic vs model-specific), and in the form their outputs take — feature attributions, surrogate models, example-based reasoning, rule extraction, and beyond.

The paper draws together the disparate threads of XAI research and identifies where the field's gaps and open problems still sit. It has since become a widely referenced entry point to the explainability literature.
