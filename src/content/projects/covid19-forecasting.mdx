## Overview

A comparative evaluation of statistical and machine-learning time-series methods applied to COVID-19 active-case data during the early pandemic, when robust baselines were thin on the ground. The paper benchmarks a panel of forecasters on percentage-of-active-cases data across countries; the notebook covers data preparation, model training and tuning, evaluation, and visualisation end to end.
