## Overview

The [Bechdel test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bechdel_test) asks whether a film has at least two named female characters who talk to each other about something other than a man. This project scrapes movie scripts from the [Internet Movie Script Database](https://www.imsdb.com/), parses dialogue and character lists with BeautifulSoup, and applies an automated version of the test at corpus scale.

Outputs a per-film pass/fail label plus aggregate statistics across the corpus (`movie_statistics.csv`).
