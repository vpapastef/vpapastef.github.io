## Overview

A survey of deep-learning architectures for multivariate time-series forecasting in IoT and smart-city contexts. Covers recurrent, convolutional, attention-based, and hybrid models; benchmarks the way they are typically evaluated; and identifies the open methodological questions the field still needs to address.
