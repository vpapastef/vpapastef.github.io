## Overview

A multi-class classifier that predicts where in the eukaryotic cell a protein operates — cytoplasm, mitochondrion, nucleus, or secreted — from features extracted from its amino-acid sequence: amino-acid composition, isoelectric point, and other physico-chemical descriptors via Biopython.

Compares ensemble methods (random forests, XGBoost) under stratified cross-validation, with predictions on a blind FASTA test set held out from training.
